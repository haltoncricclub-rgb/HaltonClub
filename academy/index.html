Age Groups

Leather-ball cricket, from U11 to U19.

Every group trains and competes at the level that's right for them, with a clear pathway upward.

U11

Foundations

First steps in leather-ball cricket — grip, stance, and throwing mechanics through games-based coaching.

U13

Development

Batting technique, pace and spin bowling basics, and fielding drills in competitive league play.

U15

Pathway Squad

For players building toward provincial trials, club-level cricket, and beyond.

U17

Advanced Squad

High-performance training and competitive league play for players approaching senior cricket.

U19

Senior Pathway

The final step before club, provincial, and national-level cricket.
